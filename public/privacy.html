Privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Who we are

Smooth Software is the trading name of Martin Hladký, an independent game developer in the Czech Republic. He is the controller of any personal data described here. For anything in this policy, write to hello@smoothsoftware.dev.

This website

smoothsoftware.dev is a static page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no accounts or forms. It is served by Cloudflare, which processes the technical data of each request (your IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Cloudflare keeps such logs briefly, as described in the Cloudflare privacy policy. Our basis for this is our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website.

Our games

Our games run in the browser, inside game portals such as CrazyGames and Poki.

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for, and we never ask for your name or email address.
  • Progress stays with you. Settings, unlocked content and the state of a game in progress are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • Anonymous statistics. To learn which parts of a game work and which devices struggle, a game may send us anonymous events: for example that a deal was finished, how long it took, and technical characteristics of your device such as its memory class and graphics chip. These events carry no identifier that could tie them to you, and we use them only in aggregate. Our basis is our legitimate interest in improving the games.
  • The portal is separate. Advertising, cookies, accounts and any login on the portal's site are handled by the portal under its own privacy policy, which you will find on that site. We receive no personal data from portals.

Email

If you write to us, we keep the correspondence for as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up, then delete it. Our mailbox is hosted by Zoho in its EU data centre, which acts as our processor.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy in a portable format. Since the site and the games collect no personal data, in practice this concerns email: write to hello@smoothsoftware.dev and we will answer within a month. You can also complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, uoou.gov.cz.

Children

Our games are suitable for all ages and collect no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top moves and the new version replaces this one at smoothsoftware.dev/privacy.
